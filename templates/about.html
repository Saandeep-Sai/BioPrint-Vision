Dataset Audit Summary
  • Total Images: 6,000 raw BMP bitmap impressions
  • Classes (8): A+ (565), A- (1009), AB+ (708), AB- (761), B+ (652), B- (741), O+ (852), O- (712)
  • Resolution: 5,956 files at 96×103 pixels, 44 files at 241×298 pixels
  • Data Integrity: 0 corrupt files; 15 identical duplicate files across 11 groups identified via MD5 hash analysis.
  • Split Ratio: Stratified random split into 70% Train (4,199), 15% Val (901), 15% Test (900).
Documented Limitations
  • Absence of Subject Identifiers: The public dataset filenames do not contain person/subject IDs. Therefore, cross-subject isolation could not be strictly guaranteed during splitting. Multi-fingerprint averaging is designed to mitigate individual impression noise.
  • Confidence Distribution: The model has a median confidence of 99.32%, meaning it is often confident even when mistaken. The application displays Model Confidence rather than diagnostic certainty.
  • Simulated Stock: Hospital availability records are internally consistent synthetic test data tagged with is_demo=True.
