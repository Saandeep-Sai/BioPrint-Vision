Fingerprint Image Quality Tips
  • Ensure even lighting without heavy cast shadows or surface glare.
  • Position the camera perpendicular to the fingerprint impressions to reduce perspective distortion.
  • Ensure papillary ridge details and bifurcations are in sharp focus.
  • Leave adequate spacing between multiple fingerprints so bounding boxes don't merge.
